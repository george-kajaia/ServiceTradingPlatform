How it works

One platform connects the companies that provide services and the customers who use them.

Issue→ Buy→ Redeemor Resell
1

Companies issue tokens

A company registers, is approved, and creates tokens for its services — setting the price, number of uses and schedule.

2

Customers buy

Customers browse the marketplace and buy tokens directly from the issuing company with a secure online payment.

3

Redeem or resell

At the point of service, staff scan the customer's QR code to redeem a use. Tokens that aren't needed can be resold.

Frequently asked questions

What is a service token?

A service token is a digital, prepaid voucher for a specific service, issued by a company on the platform. It includes a set number of uses that you redeem when you receive the service.

How do I buy a token?

Create a free account on the marketplace, browse the available tokens and pay online. The token appears in your account immediately.

How is a token redeemed?

Open the token in your account to show its QR code. The company's staff scan it with the Service Tokens scanner, and one use is redeemed instantly.

Can I resell a token?

Yes. If your plans change, you can offer your token for resale to other buyers on the platform.

How can my company start selling?

Register your company on the company portal. After our team reviews and approves the registration, you can create tokens for your services and start selling.

Is my data secure?

All connections to the platform are encrypted with TLS, and payments are processed by a dedicated payment provider — we never store your card details.
